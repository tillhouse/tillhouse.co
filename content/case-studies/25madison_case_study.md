# 25madison Venture Studio

**Role:** Fractional Product Lead  
**Company:** 25madison  
**Dates:** April 2025 – January 2026

## Overview
[25madison](https://www.25madison.com) is a New York venture studio that partners with founders and enterprises to incubate and launch new technology companies. I supported 8 AI-first B2B incubations at the 0→1 stage. They included:
- **[Ferra](https://bidferra.com)**: AI-powered structural steel estimating
- **[Mason](https://letmason.com)**: AI-native litigation platform
- **[Offload](https://letsoffload.com)**: AI-enabled heavy-haul logistics
- **[Jain](https://getjain.com)**: AI-supported commercial cleaning bid management

My depth of involvement varied by venture.

## What I did
- **Problem framing:** Shaped product vision, problem spaces, and early roadmaps to focus founders on the highest-value problems.
- **Discovery:** Led Jobs-to-be-Done customer discovery with prospective buyers and users.
- **Prototyping:** Built prototypes to reduce uncertainty before larger development investments.
- **Founder coaching:** Coached founders and cross-functional teams on modern, product-led practices and a repeatable rapid-validation method.

## What I took away
Running discovery across many ventures at once turned rapid validation into a repeatable system rather than a one-off effort. I've since applied that system in fractional engagements and at Moonfoot Labs.
