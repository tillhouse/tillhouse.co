# Analog

**Role:** Head of Product  
**Company:** Analog  
**Stage:** Early-stage fintech  
**Dates:** February 2026 – present

## Overview
Analog is building infrastructure for the regulated and conditional movement of money and value. Its first applications are in college athletics: a platform that helps athletic programs manage agreements, budgets, caps, and payouts.

I joined in February 2026 as a fractional product leader. As the company entered an early scaling phase, I moved into the full-time Head of Product role.

## What I own
- Company-wide product vision, strategy, discovery, and roadmap
- Close partnership with the founders, executive leadership, engineering, and customers
- Product validation and pilot work with university customers
- Product operating practices, and the product organization's capabilities as the company grows

## Building, not just specifying
As part of my role, I build forward-deployed products that we put in front of our pilot partners. I build them with Claude Code, deploy them on Vercel, and run the backend on Supabase. They're deployed, working software, and real customers use them and give us feedback.

That work does two jobs. It gives pilot partners something real to use early, and their feedback shows us what the platform actually needs. A core part of my role is turning those customer-specific solutions into reusable, scalable platform capabilities.

## Why it matters
Analog brings together the two halves of how I work today: executive accountability for product direction, and hands-on building with modern AI tools.
