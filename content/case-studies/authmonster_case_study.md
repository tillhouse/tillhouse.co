# AuthMonster

**Role:** Co-Founder & Head of Product  
**Company:** Moonfoot Labs  
**Status:** Live

## Overview
AuthMonster is a consumer two-factor authentication app built and launched by Moonfoot Labs. It is a brand-forward take on a category that is usually utilitarian and intimidating. The goal is to make 2FA simple and approachable for everyday people.

## My role
- Defined product strategy, positioning, and the launch plan
- Led discovery and validation with prospective users
- Built and shipped the product using AI-native development workflows

## What it shows
AuthMonster is a real, shipped consumer product, not a concept. It's a working example of the Moonfoot approach: validate quickly, build with modern AI tools, and take the product all the way to launch.
